PRIVACY

What is collected

Practice quizzes are not saved or sent anywhere.

Recorded quizzes save the following when you finish: your name, email address, association (optional), the quiz you took, your score and result, each answer you chose, and the date and time.

How it is used

  • The result is stored in a database on Cloudflare.
  • The result is emailed to the site administrator.
  • The administrator can view and download all recorded results through a password-protected dashboard.

Your information is used to record umpire assessment results. It is not used for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies or analytics. The administrator dashboard keeps its password in the browser tab only until the tab is closed.

Your information

To ask what is held about you, or to have a result corrected or deleted, contact the site administrator.
