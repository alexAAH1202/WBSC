Assessments

Your central record for every Manual Knowledge Check and Presentation Competency Assessment. Enter your details once below, then start any assessment marked as available.

Pass mark: 85% on each Presentation Competency Assessment (the marked assessment). You have a maximum of 2 attempts at each one, and the limit is checked when your result is saved. The Manual Knowledge Checks are unmarked practice: try them as often as you like, the correct answers are shown, and nothing is recorded.

Your Details

Required once per visit before starting an assessment, so your result can be recorded against you. You will confirm your email address with a one-time code. This information is only used for your completion record — it's sent to the program administrator together with your score.
