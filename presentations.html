Modules 01–05

Foundations to Appendix 4: Elite Philosophies

This single introductory session covers the Foundations Learning Guide, an overview of Appendices 1–3, and the Appendix 4 elite positioning philosophies, in five parts: the review process, the Foundations manual, Appendices 1–3, Appendix 4 elite mechanics, and rule changes & integrity. Presented by members of the WBSC Umpires Commission Mechanics Working Group, including Leigh Evans and Frankie Billingsley.

Softball Australia Guidance

Mechanics Bulletin MB-2026-1 applies this framework within the Softball Australia pathway: Elite mechanics (Appendices 4 and 5) are used at U18, U23 and Open National Championships, and are assessed for umpires at Levels 5 and 6. Umpires at Levels 1–4 continue to be trained and assessed on Foundational mechanics.

Other Federations should confirm the equivalent guidance for their own accreditation pathway.
