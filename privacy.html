Privacy

What this temporary training site collects, why, who can see it, and how long it is kept.

Who is responsible

This is a temporary training site, not the official WBSC website. The Softball Umpire Mechanics program is run by the WBSC Umpires Commission. The person responsible for the information described on this page is Leigh Evans, Director of Umpires, who can be contacted at umpiredirector@wbscoceania.org.

What is collected

To take a marked Presentation Competency Assessment you enter your full name, email address, and optionally your Umpire ID and association or federation. We confirm that you control the email address by sending you a one-time code.

When you finish a marked assessment, this site records your name, verified email address, Umpire ID and association (if given), the module and assessment, your score and pass or not-passed result, which attempt it was, the date and time, and the program version. Nothing is recorded as a result while you are still answering questions. If you stop a marked assessment part-way, the question you reached, your score so far, which questions you were given and the order of the answer options are saved so you can carry on, including from another device (see “Where it goes” and “How long it is kept”).

The Manual Knowledge Checks are practice only. You do not enter any details for them and nothing about them is recorded.

Emails we send

We email you a login code, and after each marked attempt a receipt showing your result and attempt number, in the language the site was showing when you asked. The Director of Umpires receives a notice of each result and a notice when someone has passed all eight modules, and every Monday a summary of progress: who completed the program that week, who has used every attempt on a module without passing, who started but has had no activity for 14 days, and totals by association or federation. These are service emails about your training record. We do not send marketing or newsletters, and we do not email learners reminders.

Why we use it, and our basis

To confirm who took each assessment, apply the pass mark and the limit of two attempts, and keep a record that you completed the 2026-2029 accreditation and reaccreditation program. The basis is the legitimate interest of WBSC and its member federations in running and recording that program, and it is not used for advertising or sold. You can object or ask questions at any time.

Where it goes and who can see it

Results are saved as a row in a Google Sheet managed by the program administrator, and emails are sent through Gmail. The site uses Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Gmail, so Google processes this information for us and may do so on servers outside your country. A login code (10 minutes) and a login session (6 hours) are held temporarily in Google Apps Script’s short-term cache and then expire; the code is kept only as a one-way hash, not as plain text. If you stop a marked assessment part-way, its progress is saved on a separate tab of the same Google Sheet. It is stored against a one-way hash of your email address, not the address itself, and holds only the questions drawn, the question you reached, your score so far, the order of the answer options and the questions you have missed so far.

Access is limited to the program administrator and the Director of Umpires. The Director can view results on a password-protected page. Results are not published or shared with other organisations by this site.

How long it is kept

Results are kept for the length of the 2026-2029 program cycle and for two years after it ends, that is until the end of 2031. After that they are deleted, or kept only as anonymous totals that do not identify anyone. The notification emails sent to the Director of Umpires are deleted on the same schedule. Saved progress from an unfinished attempt is ignored after 24 hours and deleted after 48 hours, and is deleted as soon as you finish or choose to start again. Emails in your own inbox are yours to keep or delete.

Cookies and what is kept on your device

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking. The fonts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact Google or any other third party to load a page.

While you are using the site, your browser keeps the details you typed and your login session in its session storage for that visit only; it is cleared when you close the tab. After you press OK on the cookies notice, or choose a language, that choice is remembered in your browser so that the notice is not shown again and the site opens in your language. If you stop a marked assessment part-way, your progress (the questions you were given, the question you reached, your score so far and the order of the answer options) is also kept in your browser for up to 24 hours so you can resume on the same device, and, once your email is confirmed, with Google as described above so you can resume on another device; it is removed when you finish or choose to start again. So that a second attempt at a marked assessment asks different questions where the assessment has a larger pool, your browser also remembers which questions your last attempt used, together with your email address, until you clear your browser data. That is all this site stores on your device. If you share a computer, choose “Start again” or clear your browser data.

When you request a login code or save a result, your browser sends the request to Google. Google receives technical information such as your IP address and may set its own cookies when it does; this is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy. The manuals and presentations are held on Google Drive and open in a new tab; Google handles those pages under its own policies.

Security

Results are only recorded after your email address has been confirmed, the pass mark and attempt limit are applied on the server, and the Director’s results page needs a password. No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects your information, the Director of Umpires will tell the people affected and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, ask for a result to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used. Contact Leigh Evans, Director of Umpires, at umpiredirector@wbscoceania.org. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the privacy regulator in your own country or state.
