Softball Australia Guidance

Mechanics Bulletin MB-2026-1 applies this framework within the Softball Australia pathway: Elite mechanics are used at U18, U23 and Open National Championships, and are assessed for umpires at Levels 5 and 6. Umpires at Levels 1–4 continue to be trained and assessed on Foundational mechanics; State Directors of Umpiring determine whether Elite mechanics also apply to state-based competitions.

Other Federations should confirm the equivalent guidance for their own accreditation pathway.
